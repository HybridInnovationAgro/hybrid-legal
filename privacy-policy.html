Privacy Policy

Effective 16 September 2026 · applies to Hybrid Innovation Agro Ltd's TikTok content-publishing integration

Who we are

Hybrid Innovation Agro Ltd ("we", "us", "our"), company number 14817937, registered at 4 Cheshire Road, Maidstone, England, ME15 8HN, operates an internal content-publishing tool that posts video content to our own TikTok account on our behalf. This policy explains what that tool does with data obtained through TikTok's platform.

Scope

This policy covers only our use of the TikTok API in connection with our internal automation ("the integration"), built as part of our content operation. It does not cover our website generally, or any other product we may operate.

What the integration does

The integration publishes pre-approved video content to a TikTok account we control, automatically, as part of our own workflow. Every video is prepared and approved internally before it is queued for publishing. No content is generated or published from any TikTok user's data — only from material we create ourselves.

Information we obtain from TikTok

When we connect our TikTok account to the integration, we obtain, and store, only what is required to authenticate and publish on that account's behalf:

  • An OAuth access token and refresh token for the connected account
  • The TikTok user ID and username of the connected account
  • Publish confirmations returned by the Content Posting API (e.g. the ID of a successfully posted video)

We do not request or read analytics, comments, follower or following lists, direct messages, or any data belonging to other TikTok users or viewers. The integration uses the Content Posting API to publish video only — nothing is read back from the platform beyond confirming that a post succeeded.

How we use this information

Solely to authenticate the connected account with TikTok and to publish the video content described above. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to operating the integration.

Storage and security

Access and refresh tokens are stored on infrastructure we control, and access is limited to the personnel who operate the integration. Tokens are retained only for as long as the TikTok account remains connected.

Disconnecting and deletion

The connected account's owner may revoke the integration's access at any time from TikTok's own app permissions settings. Once access is revoked, we delete the stored access and refresh tokens for that account within 30 days. To request earlier deletion, or to ask what data we hold, contact us at velmariscapital@gmail.com.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share the information described above with third parties. It is not used to build advertising profiles, and it is not shared with any party outside Hybrid Innovation Agro Ltd.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the integration accesses or how we handle it, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
