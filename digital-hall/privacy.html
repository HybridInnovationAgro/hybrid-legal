Privacy Policy — Digital Hall

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Who we are

“Digital Hall” is a private internal application used only by its owner, Roza Truksane, to connect her own Gmail mailboxes (Golden Seller and Velmaris Capital) to her own workflow automation (n8n). It is not offered to the public and has no users other than its owner.

Google user data we access

When the owner connects a mailbox, the app receives access to that mailbox through Google OAuth with these Gmail scopes: https://mail.google.com/, gmail.modify, gmail.compose, gmail.labels, gmail.addons.current.message.action and gmail.addons.current.action.compose. We also store the OAuth tokens needed to keep the connection working.

How we use it

In practice the app reads recent messages of the owner’s own mailboxes (sender, subject, text and attachments’ names) so that the owner can review business correspondence and receive summaries. Any reply or draft is created only by the owner’s own decision. Data is used only to provide this function to the owner.

Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell Google user data, do not use it for advertising, do not use it to train generalized AI models, and do not transfer it to third parties except as needed to provide the function above, for security, or where required by law. No person reads the mailbox content except the owner.

Storage and security

Mail content is processed on the owner’s own server and kept only as long as needed for the business purpose. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted in the owner’s n8n installation.

Your rights and deletion

The owner can revoke the app’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the stored tokens and any copied mail data are then deleted. Anyone whose email is in these mailboxes may ask for access or deletion by writing to the contact below; we reply within 30 days.
